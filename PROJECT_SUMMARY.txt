📋 NOTIFICATION FLOW

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ User adds item dengan deadline: 2024-02-22 15:00           │
│ + notification enabled (30 menit)                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                           ↓
        CardDetailViewModel.onEvent(AddItem)
                           ↓
        checklistRepository.addItem(item)
                           ↓
    (Jika notification enabled)
    alarmScheduler.scheduleItemNotifications()
                           ↓
        ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
        │ Schedule 2 alarms:                   │
        │ 1. 14:30 (30 min before deadline)  │
        │ 2. 15:00 (at deadline)              │
        └──────────────────────────────────────┘
                           ↓
    AlarmManager.setExactAndAllowWhileIdle()
                           ↓
        [Sistem akan trigger pada waktu]
                           ↓
        BroadcastReceiver (AlarmReceiver)
                           ↓
    NotificationManager.showNotification()
                           ↓
        User lihat notif di notification tray
                           ↓
        Tap notif → buka app ke card detail
